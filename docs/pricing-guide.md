# Palate — Pricing Guide

## Philosophy

Palate is sold to the **host** (the venue, sommelier, or event planner), not to individual guests. Pricing should reflect the value the host gets: a better experience for their guests, post-event analytics, and a tool that replaces paper scoring cards and manual tracking.

The model that works best at this stage is **per-event with a monthly subscription option**. Per-event pricing removes friction for first-time buyers (no long-term commitment). Monthly subscriptions reward recurring hosts with a lower effective cost per event.

---

## Tiers

### Starter — Free
*Try it with no risk*

- 1 active event at a time
- Up to 20 guests
- Basic wine rating (1–5 stars) + tasting notes
- Simple results view after the event
- No analytics export, no custom branding
- Booth mode only

**Who it's for:** A wine bar wanting to try it for one small event. A sommelier testing the product before recommending it to a client. A private host running a dinner party.

**Goal:** Get venues using it. 2–5% of free users convert to paid — but 100% of paid users started somewhere.

---

### Host — $149/event &nbsp;or&nbsp; $99/month
*For independent venues running regular tastings*

- Up to 75 guests per event
- Booth mode + Wine Crawl mode
- Tasting Buddy matching
- Basic analytics: top-rated wines, average scores, engagement summary
- PDF export of results
- Email support

**Annual option:** $79/month ($948/year) — save 20%

**Who it's for:** Independent wine bars (Volo, Rootstock, Pompette), restaurants adding a monthly tasting series, boutique event planners running private tastings.

**Pitch:** At one event per month, the monthly plan costs $99. That's less than a case of wine and gives every guest a more memorable, interactive experience — with data to improve the next one.

---

### Pro — $349/event &nbsp;or&nbsp; $199/month
*For venues and professionals running larger or more frequent events*

Everything in Host, plus:
- Up to 250 guests per event
- Custom event branding (logo, colors, welcome message)
- Full analytics dashboard (guest engagement, wine comparison charts, session timeline)
- Priority support (same-day response)
- Multiple concurrent events

**Annual option:** $159/month ($1,908/year) — save 20%

**Who it's for:** City Winery, The Sommelier Company, Brix Catering, Lacuna Events running medium-scale tastings. Venues that run events more than 4× per month where monthly is clearly better than per-event.

**Pitch:** A sommelier company running 2–3 corporate events per month pays $199 instead of $700–$1,047 in per-event fees. The analytics report becomes a deliverable they hand to their corporate client.

---

### Venue / Enterprise — Custom pricing
*For large-scale events, multi-venue festivals, or white-label needs*

Everything in Pro, plus:
- Unlimited guests
- Multi-venue / festival crawl mode (guests move between stations)
- White-label or co-branded experience (your logo, your colors, no Palate branding)
- Custom event setup and onboarding
- Dedicated point of contact
- Annual contract

**Starting at:** $500/event or $3,600/year

**Who it's for:** Lacuna Events, Chicago Winery (large-scale corporate), hotel groups running quarterly wine programs, multi-venue wine crawl festivals (e.g., a River North or West Loop wine walk).

**Pitch:** One large event (200+ guests, multi-station) at a competing platform like Guidebook or Eventify runs $1,500–$3,750. Palate is purpose-built for wine — not a generic event app adapted for tastings.

## Why Not Per-Attendee Pricing?

Per-attendee pricing (e.g., $2/guest) sounds fair but creates problems:
- **Unpredictable cost** — a host can't finalize event budget until RSVPs close
- **Friction at sign-up** — "how many people are coming?" is a bad first question
- **Penalizes success** — a popular event costs more, which feels wrong

Flat per-event or monthly fees are how the best comparable tools price (Guidebook, Perfect Venue, CellarPass higher tiers). It builds trust.

## Suggested Sales Motion

1. **Offer the free tier first.** Ask if they have an event coming up in the next 30 days. Help them set it up at no cost.
2. **Follow up after the event.** Share what the analytics would have shown if they'd been on Host or Pro. Let the data sell itself.
3. **Convert to monthly** once they've run 2–3 paid events. The math is obvious: 2 events/month on Host tier = $298 per event vs. $99/month.
4. **Upsell to Enterprise** when a venue books a large event (100+ guests) or asks about removing Palate branding.

## Early Beta / Founding Client Offer

For the first 10 clients acquired through direct outreach:
- 3 months of Pro tier at $99/month (Host price, Pro features)
- In exchange: a short testimonial and agreement to be listed as a reference
- This creates anchoring — they know the real price is $199/month and they're getting a deal
